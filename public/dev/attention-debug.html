Vertical lid gap ÷ horizontal eye width, averaged over both eyes.

What you are looking at

  • Cyan lines are the EAR measurement: the vertical pair (159-145, 386-374) over the horizontal pair (33-133, 362-263). Close your eyes slowly and watch the ratio collapse.
  • Amber solid lines are yaw method A, the production one: nose 1 to the face contour at 234 and 454. Those points sit at your temples.
  • Indigo dashed lines are yaw method B, the candidate: nose 1 to the outer eye corners 33 and 263. Rest your hands on your head and watch A jump while B stays put — occlusion at the temples drags the contour landmarks, but not the eye corners. That is the false positive you found, on screen.
  • Pink marks the iris centroid (468-472, 473-477) against the eye-corner span. Keep your head still and look sideways — only this one should move.
  • The EAR check has a debounce: it needs the ratio held below threshold for EYES_CLOSED_MS before firing, which is what stops ordinary blinking from scoring as distraction. Set the slider to 0 and blink to see the difference.
  • There is no pitch detection. Look straight down while keeping your head square to the camera — every check still passes. That is a real blind spot in the production tracker, not a bug in this page.
