Face-landmark engines — head-to-head benchmark

Runs every realistic in-browser alternative against the same camera frames on the same hardware, then checks which ones can actually produce the three signals NextLearn's attention score needs: eye openness (EAR), head pose (yaw) and gaze direction.

Privacy: every engine runs locally in this browser. No frame, image or landmark is uploaded or stored. The only network traffic is fetching the model weights from their CDNs.
What this measures and what it does not. It measures speed (latency, FPS), capability (landmark count → which signals are derivable) and detection rate on your frames. It does not measure landmark accuracy — that needs a hand-annotated ground-truth set, which this project does not have. Any accuracy claim would be unfounded, so none is made.

Engines

Results

Latency is the end-to-end inference call per frame. The app throttles to one frame every 125 ms (8 fps); "budget" is the share of that consumed at p95. Signals are derived from the measured landmark count, not assumed.
